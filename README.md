One newspaper front page summing up the world's news of Tuesday, 17 November 2026: "THE WORLD — TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 17, 2026". Five stories, each with its own illustration, headline and sub-headline, so the page explains itself to a reader with no news feed: the Crimea bridge strike (Ukraine collapses the span; Russia hits Kyiv's bridges), Trump's Asia trip (Manila summit, then talks with Xi and Putin), tankers hit by mines in the Strait of Hormuz as a third U.S. carrier arrives, the Vulcan rocket failure that destroyed 40 Amazon satellites, and the JPMorgan outage blamed on a software bug. `main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) validates the bundled 1024x1024 PNG and copies it to the output as `entry.png` — no network, no randomness, two runs byte-identical (527,931 bytes).

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
